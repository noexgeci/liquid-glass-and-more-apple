Process

Four stages. No shortcuts.

  1. Inspect

    We read paint depth on every panel before a pad touches it.

    12 readings per panel · ±1 µm

  2. Decontaminate

    Iron remover, clay and a pH-neutral wash take off what polish would grind in.

    pH 7 · 3 wash buckets

    FAQ

    Asked at every drop-off.

    Does correction thin my clear coat?
    A little, and we measure it. We remove about 3 µm on average and stop well before any panel drops below 90 µm of clear coat.
    How long do you keep the car?
    Refresh is same-day. Correction takes two days, Signature three, including the 24-hour ceramic cure indoors.
    Can I wash it myself afterwards?
    Yes, from day eight. Use the two-bucket method and a pH-neutral shampoo; we send you home with both.
    What does the warranty cover?
    Loss of gloss or water beading on the coated paint for nine years, with a yearly inspection. It transfers when you sell the car.
